# Monthly refresh is incremental and excludes trials that age out of scope

The initial load is a full fetch/parse/graph-build of all recruiting/active trials. The monthly refresh instead: (a) fetches and Cortex-parses only trials whose `lastUpdatePostDate` is newer than the last run, reusing the existing `PARSING_PROGRESS` tracking tables; (b) `MERGE`s new/changed trial and criteria data into the existing Neo4j graph rather than rebuilding it, so search stays live throughout; and (c) detects trials whose status has moved outside `RECRUITING`/`ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING` and excludes them from the graph. We rejected full monthly reprocessing (real recurring LLM-inference cost at ~86K-trial scale for no new information) and rejected leaving aged-out trials in place (a physician could be matched to a trial no longer accepting patients).
